# Cyber Sentinel Name and Branding Policy

This policy governs identification of the official Cyber-Sentinel-Atlas project. It is not a statement that any mark is registered in any jurisdiction.

You may accurately refer to the project by name, cite it, link to the official repository, and state that independent work is compatible with or derived from Atlas when that statement is truthful.

You may not use the Cyber Sentinel / Cyber-Sentinel-Atlas name, logos, release badges or other official branding in a way that is likely to imply that an unofficial fork, product, service, training course or distribution is maintained, endorsed, certified or released by the official project unless the maintainer has explicitly authorized that representation.

Forks should use a clearly distinct project/distribution name and should identify the upstream relationship. Unmodified attribution and source notices should be preserved where required.

The official repository, official release tags and maintainer-approved distribution channels are the authority for what constitutes an official Atlas release.
